ABOUT US

\--------



UNDERSCORE LIMITED is a software development initiative focused on building useful, innovative, accessible, and user-friendly digital products.



Our goal is to make technology more practical, more accessible, and more enjoyable for everyone. We aim to create software that solves real problems, simplifies everyday tasks, encourages creativity, and gives users powerful tools without unnecessary complexity.



We believe that great software should not be limited to large corporations or expensive platforms. Everyone deserves access to useful digital experiences and tools that help them learn, create, work, and explore.



OUR MOTTO

\---------



"Code. Create. Innovate."



Every product we build represents our commitment to creativity, continuous improvement, and meaningful innovation.



OUR MISSION

\-----------



Our mission is to design, develop, and deliver software that makes everyday digital experiences better.



We aim to:



1\. Build useful desktop applications and digital tools.

2\. Make technology accessible to as many people as possible.

3\. Prioritize user experience, simplicity, performance, and reliability.

4\. Develop lightweight software that works well on a wide range of devices.

5\. Encourage creativity, learning, reading, writing, coding, and entertainment.

6\. Make our products approachable for beginners while remaining useful to experienced users.

7\. Explore new ideas and turn them into practical software.

8\. Respect user privacy and minimize unnecessary data collection.

9\. Provide free access to our products wherever feasible.

10\. Continuously improve our software based on testing, feedback, and new ideas.



OUR VISION

\----------



Our vision is to grow UNDERSCORE LIMITED into a trusted software brand with a diverse ecosystem of interconnected digital products.



We want to create an environment where users can read books, write manuscripts, browse the internet, develop software, enjoy games, and accomplish everyday digital tasks through products designed with care.



We envision a future in which our products are recognized for their usability, thoughtful design, accessibility, and commitment to users.



OUR PRODUCTS

\------------



UNDERSCORE LIMITED is developing an ecosystem of software products, including:



uBrowser

A privacy-conscious web browser designed to make browsing convenient, customizable, and enjoyable.



uReader

A dedicated reading application designed for novels, books, and PDF documents.



uAuthor

A writing studio intended to help authors create, organize, and publish their manuscripts.



UminiTV

A lightweight entertainment hub that brings supported online entertainment services and useful shortcuts together in one application.



uDev

A development environment designed to make coding and software creation more approachable.



U Games

A gaming hub intended to bring together built-in games, community-submitted games, and links to games created by other developers.



These products represent our broader ambition to build a connected, useful, and accessible software ecosystem. Features and availability may evolve as development progresses.



(although some products might not be published yet they are under progress and will be completed soon and some more products are on their way!!)

OUR APPROACH TO SOFTWARE

\------------------------



At UNDERSCORE LIMITED, software development is more than writing code. It involves understanding problems, planning solutions, testing carefully, improving the user experience, and maintaining products responsibly.



We believe that software should be:



\- Useful rather than unnecessarily complicated.

\- Designed with users in mind.

\- Efficient without sacrificing essential functionality.

\- Transparent about its capabilities and limitations.

\- Maintained and improved as time and resources permit.

\- Built with respect for security, privacy, and intellectual property.



We also recognize that every ambitious project begins with a first version. We value steady progress, practical decisions, and learning throughout the development process.



OUR COMMITMENT TO FREE ACCESS

\-----------------------------



We believe that access to useful software and knowledge should be as broad as possible.



Where feasible, we aim to offer our products free of charge and explore ways to share resources across our product ecosystem.



For example, uAuthor is intended to support authors in creating and publishing their work, while uReader aims to help readers access books and manuscripts. Our goal is to make the connection between creating and reading digital books more accessible.



Any distribution of books, software, or other content will respect applicable copyright, licensing, and author permissions.



OUR LONG-TERM GOALS

\-------------------



Our long-term goals include:



\- Developing and maintaining a reliable family of software products.

\- Improving the quality, stability, and performance of existing applications.

\- Expanding compatibility across supported devices and operating systems.

\- Building stronger connections between our products.

\- Encouraging independent creators and community participation.

\- Making useful digital tools accessible to more people.

\- Exploring new software categories as our capabilities grow.

\- Building a recognizable and trusted software identity.

\- Creating products that remain useful beyond their initial release.
